Questions, answered

Is this the same report as CARFAX®?

On the records that decide a purchase — yes, the same core facts. Federal law requires every history provider, CarFax included, to draw title brands, odometer data and salvage records from the DOJ's NMVTIS database, and recalls/specs come from NHTSA for everyone. What CarFax sells on top is their private accident & service-record network, whose depth varies by vehicle. VINwise gives you the same federal core, NMVTIS-aligned history insights, and adds what they don't: 6 live local comps and a marketplace proof card — as a Comprehensive Public & Market Value Insights Report. We never resell or claim to be their proprietary report.

Where does the data come from?

Specifications and recalls: the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's free public vPIC database. History insights: NMVTIS-aligned public records via approved data partners. Local comps: aggregated live marketplace listings near you. In this demo build, history and comps use clearly-labeled simulated data until you connect live providers (one config flag each).

Is $15 really one-time?

Yes. One flat micro-transaction per full report. No subscription, no upsells, no stored card. Checkout is processed entirely by Stripe or PayPal — we never see your payment details (PCI-DSS SAQ-A).

How are local comps calculated?

We match your decoded year/make/model/trim and reported mileage against comparable vehicles currently or recently listed in your area, then position each on price-per-mile curves to surface a fair-value band (25th–75th percentile). It's the same comp logic real-estate apps use — finally applied to cars.

Our data commitments

  • ●We clearly label every data source. Public government data is called public government data.
  • ●History insights are sourced in alignment with the National Motor Vehicle Title Information System (NMVTIS). NMVTIS data is reported by state DMVs, insurance carriers, and junk/salvage yards — not all entities report, and absence of a record is not a guarantee that no damage occurred. Always inspect independently before purchase.
  • ●Valuations are estimates generated from comparable listings and depreciation models — not appraisals.
  • ●Simulated/demo fields are always badged “SAMPLE”. We never dress mock data up as purchased facts.
